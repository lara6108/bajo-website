How to
drink Bajo

Simple rituals for the moments that matter.

I

Neat, over a single cube

The purest way to meet Bajo. One large ice cube, a wide glass. Let the lavender open as it chills. Sip slowly — there's nowhere to be.

The Classic
II

With tonic & a twist

60ml Bajo over ice, topped with premium tonic, finished with a curl of orange peel. Bright, floral, effortless. The glass that starts every good evening.

The Signature
III

The Bajo Margarita

60ml Bajo, fresh lime, agave syrup, shaken with ice. Rim with Tajín if you're feeling it. Pour for friends. Repeat.

The Crowd-Pleaser
IV

Hibiscus Spritz

Bajo with hibiscus syrup, a splash of sparkling water, and fresh mint. Pink, stunning, and completely alive. The one everyone asks about.
